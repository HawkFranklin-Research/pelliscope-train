# Original manuscript claim

The original 10-condition manuscript introduced the comparison in `AIIM/company_manuscript.tex`:

- Line 280: Google/Derm Foundation embeddings with Random Forest achieved macro/micro ROC-AUCs of 0.797/0.854.
- Lines 282–290: Figure 2C compared six encoders and six classifiers.
- Line 288: the figure caption identified Google Derm plus MIL as the highest mean macro ROC-AUC at 0.819.
- Lines 292–296: the Results described an absolute macro ROC-AUC improvement of 0.022 and linked it to case-level multi-image inference.
- Lines 348–356: the Discussion interpreted the improvement as modest evidence that multi-photo case structure contained useful information.

Reviewer 2 later asked that the 0.797 to 0.819 improvement be recognized as a limitation until statistical significance and external reproducibility were established.

The present 25-condition analysis reaches a similar question. It therefore reports matched case-level uncertainty, ranking metrics, prevalence-restricted sensitivity analyses, and the complete model grid without claiming that the most favorable point estimate proves MIL superiority.
